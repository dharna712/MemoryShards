How the pieces get found.

Five stages. Each one shows the bar it has to clear.

STAGE 01

Extract

Reads time and GPS out of the photo file itself.

THE BAR

29 of 42 real photos gave a full time and place.

STAGE 02

Cluster

Groups photos into events by time and place.

THE BAR

Recovered both fabricated events from 7 photos.

STAGE 03

Recognize

Finds every face and groups them by person.

THE BAR

94% verification on people it never saw, up from 59% before fine-tuning.

STAGE 04

Caption

Describes each event in a sentence.

THE BAR

Pretrained BLIP, inference only. Not trained by us, and we say so.

STAGE 05

Reconstruct

Fuses events, places and people into one timeline.

THE BAR

6 ordered events from 11 real photos, end to end.
