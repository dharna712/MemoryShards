How the pieces get found

Four stages, each one a real deep-learning or signal-processing step — not just sorting files by date.

1

Extract

Pull EXIF timestamp + GPS from photos; timestamp-parse any text notes or messages.

2

Cluster

Group items into discrete events by time + location proximity (DBSCAN over a joint time/geo space).

3

Caption

A pretrained vision-language model (BLIP/CLIP) describes what's happening in each event's photos.

4

Reconstruct

Fuse captioned events and parsed notes into one ordered, human-readable timeline.
